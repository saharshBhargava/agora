
Mobile App: One stop app to manage all your purchases across online platforms and in-person.

Users take a video of items in their house including:
- Food
- Furniture
- Toiletries
- Hobby Items 

We use Perceptron's  models to generate lists of products that users already purchase and use regularly from the videos they take. Our app will build an inventory and preferences list from the information we get from the videos and any user input. 

We can group items in the inventory in a few categories, including item type, the store they were purchased from, and more. 

by store they are from and track buy frequency/ consumption rate per item. 

In our inventory, we can also track user consumption rates and product requirements. Based on the user's needs, our app can add trips to stores at the best available time on their Google Calendar. Our app will also store shopping lists per store.

Our app should also track items that the user shortlists for price changes and discounts and alert the user when shortlisted items are on sale or reduced prices. Our app should also recommend items to users based on their pre-existing preferences for other items. Users can also take photos or videos of products while shopping. Our app identifies the items and compares prices across their preferred stores and websites. 

Our app will have several layers of autonomy:
- Let agents buy items
- Let them add items to your online cart(s)
- Agents can present items/information about items to users.
