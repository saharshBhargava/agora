VISA Judging Rubric
| Criteria | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Innovation | 25% |
| Technology Execution | 20% |
| User Experience | 20% |
| Commerce Impact | 15% |
| Trust & Responsibility | 15% |
Innovation (25%) – How creatively does the solution reimagine the shopping experience and bring together the right “ingredients”?
User Experience (25%) – Does the experience feel intuitive, useful, seamless, and polished under pressure?
Technical Execution (20%) – How effectively has the team implemented AI and supporting technologies?
Commerce Impact (15%) – Can the solution improve customer engagement, conversion, loyalty, speed, or merchant outcomes?
Trust & Responsibility (15%) – Does the solution address privacy, transparency, security, and responsible use of AI?
